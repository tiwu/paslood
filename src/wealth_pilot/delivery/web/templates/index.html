🇧🇪 Regional Tax & Benchmark Preferences

Configure default fiscal parameters for the Capital Gains Tax Simulator on the Guidance tab.

ℹ️ Why is this here & what does it add?

Under the Belgian 2026 Capital Gains Tax (Meerwaardebelasting) framework, historical gains accrued before Dec 31, 2025 are tax-exempt: shares bought before 2026 use their Dec 31, 2025 closing price ("Foto-moment") as their fiscal cost basis rather than original purchase price. Realized gains above the €10,000 annual exemption are taxed at 10%. Setting your defaults here pre-fills the Tax Simulator in the Guidance tab and informs the AI Advisor when planning tax-free rebalancing sales.

ℹ️ Wealth Pilot Guidance & Help

🪣 Strategic Allocation Buckets

Unlike budgeting apps that track individual coffees, Wealth Pilot organizes your wealth into macro allocation pillars (Core World ETFs, Tech, Gold, Cash Buffer). Each bucket has a target weight (%) and drift tolerance (e.g. ±3.0%).

💡 Smart Buy-Only Rebalancing

Instead of selling your winning shares (which incurs transaction costs and taxes), Wealth Pilot calculates how to allocate your monthly incoming cashflow to underweight buckets, gradually bringing your portfolio back to target.

📂 Ingestion Channels

Upload your KBC CSV export directly in the Cashflow tab. The parser automatically detects your checking balance, mortgage payments, and recurring expenses. Investment positions can be ingested via PDT MCP or Generic CSV.
